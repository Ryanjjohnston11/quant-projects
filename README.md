## Current Projects

### 1. Topological Market Regimes
**Status:** Active (July 2026 – )

Application of topological data analysis (persistent homology) to financial time series for regime detection and structural change identification.

- Rolling-window embeddings of equity returns
- Persistent homology (H₀ / H₁) via Vietoris–Rips filtrations
- Extraction of topological features (total persistence, Betti curves, persistence entropy, Wasserstein distances)
- Comparison against major market events and traditional volatility/correlation signals

**Goal:** Test whether topological summaries of market geometry provide useful, non-redundant information for regime classification and early warning of structural breaks.

→ [`/tda-market-regimes`](./tda-market-regimes)
